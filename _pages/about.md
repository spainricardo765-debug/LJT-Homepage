## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group, led by my supervisor Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
